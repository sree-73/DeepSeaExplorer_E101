# DeepSeaExplorer_E101
Deep Sea Explorer is an educational computer game developed by our team for our First Year Engineering Design Day (FEDD) Project at NCSU. Designed for middle schoolers, the game encourages curiosity about marine health and biodiversity through an immersive exploration experience. It proudly won first place at FEDD Day.

🌊 **Features**
-  Progressive levels that take players deeper into the ocean, discovering unique marine creatures. 
-  Interactive quizzes designed to educate and foster appreciation for marine life and environmental health.
-  A user-friendly interface and colorful original artwork to ensure accessibility for younger audiences.
